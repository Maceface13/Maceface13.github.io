Hello, world: why I'm starting this blog

October 27, 2026

I borrowed the title from the first program almost everyone writes. It prints two words and proves the setup works. This post is the same idea, for a blog.

I'm a student, and I want a place to write about that. When I learn something interesting, I want to put it in my own words. Writing it down helps me see whether I actually understand it, and gives me something to look back on later.

Some posts will be about classes: math, accounting, tax accounting, or wherever I end up if I pivot. Some will just be a small thing I noticed and wanted to keep. I don't have a schedule. If a week is quiet, the blog can be quiet too.

I'm keeping this humble on purpose. Short and honest is better than long and impressive. If I explain something poorly, I can edit the page later.

Thanks for reading the first one. The page works. Next time, I'll write about something I learned.
